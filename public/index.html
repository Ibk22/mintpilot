Revenue

Estimated lifetime mint revenue, derived on chain: paid mints (total minted minus the free first mints each contract reports) multiplied by the contract's current mint price. Mint revenue is swept out of the contracts by withdraw(), so this is a lifetime estimate, not a balance. Secondary-sale royalties are paid by marketplaces straight to the payout address and are not counted. Collections whose contracts do not expose the price and free-mint counters show “n/a” and are left out of the totals.
